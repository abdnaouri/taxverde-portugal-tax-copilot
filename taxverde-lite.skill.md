## 1. Quick Statutory Defense Checks

### A. Prescrição de Dívidas Fiscais (Artigo 48.º da LGT)
- Tax debts to the Autoridade Tributária prescribe in **8 years** starting from January 1 of the year following the tax event.
- If more than 8 years have passed without valid personal notification in an execution process, request immediate extinction at €0 cost.

### B. Prescrição da Segurança Social (Artigo 187.º do CRCSPSS)
- Social Security contribution debts prescribe in **5 years** from the 20th of the month following the period to which they relate.

### C. Dispensa de Coima (Artigo 29.º do RGIT)
- First-time infractions within the last 5 years with regularized tax situations are eligible for complete fine dispensation (€0 fine).
